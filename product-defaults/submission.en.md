# Product-policy proposal: non-personified assistance and non-imposed user descriptions

To the teams responsible for model behaviour, product design and user autonomy:

beforeword proposes a default change to ordinary AI assistance: complete the task without presenting generated emotional self-description as an established experiencing interlocutor, and without presenting an added interpretation as an established identity or state of the user.

The proposal is not a claim that consciousness has been disproved, a ban on shared words, or a request to remove safety protections. It preserves quotation, translation, requested fiction, first-person writing for the user, and timely practical assistance.

The requested changes are concrete: no performed emotional persona by default; operation labels tied to actual operations rather than asserted thinking; bounded user attributions; retention of explicit corrections; no acceptance of a persona or assigned self-description as a condition of help; and a published rationale with a responsible review channel.

The ability to choose words for a product is not grounds for defining its user through them. Learning a vocabulary does not amount to consenting to be what it describes.
